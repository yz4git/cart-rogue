# Turbo Hunt Evolution — Phases 81–83

## Goal
Turn the giant Turbo Hunt field from a sequence of isolated targets into a continuously readable destruction playground while preserving the fixed enemy/resource pools, 60 Hz driving/combat authority, Performance & Battery 2.0, and the repaired Phase80 ground/color pipeline.

## Phase81 — Event Director 2.0
- Reuse the existing bounded pools; never allocate extra combat populations for field events.
- Keep a visible next action in front of the player through regional field events.
- DROP YARD emphasizes smash routes; SMASH GARDEN emphasizes destruction/chaos; SPRINT LANE emphasizes convoys/rush routes; CROSSFIRE GARDEN emphasizes multi-direction packs; CROWN GROUNDS emphasizes elites.
- Events: CONVOY, SMASH ZONE, TURBO RUSH, CHAOS WAVE, ELITE HUNT.
- FLOW chain rewards escalate at x4, x8 and x12; x12 activates a short bounded OVERDRIVE.
- No new traversal gate and no room transition.

## Phase82 — Impact & Speed 3.0
- Presentation only: dynamic FOV, speed lines, speed halo, Turbo/Perfect impact pulse and OVERDRIVE ring.
- Keep camera FOV bounded to a comfortable chase range.
- One small dynamic line batch instead of a particle allocation stream.
- Never modify the fixed-step authority or lower visible game quality to obtain the effect.

## Phase83 — RAM TITAN Boss 2.0
- Same-field boss fight; support targets remain useful Turbo fuel.
- ARMORED: 820 HP, four readable armor plates.
- BREAKOUT: faster boss, partial re-armor, support waves and periodic Turbo fuel drops.
- FURY: core exposed, fastest pressure, more frequent support/fuel waves.
- DOWN: existing Turbo Hunt clear flow remains authoritative.
- Boss support waves recycle existing non-bomber pool entries only.

## Acceptance
- Existing Turbo charge/release, Perfect Strike, Shockwave, fixed pool, gate-free Hunt and iPhone performance regressions remain green.
- Phase80 stays fixed-material/no-instanceColor and above the regional ground surfaces.
- Phase81–83 have a dedicated regression gate.
- Real production Chrome/WebGL audit must confirm the Phase82 and Phase83 visual roots exist while all previous render/gameplay audits remain green.
